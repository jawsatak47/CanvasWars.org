      What Is CanvasWars?

      Three rules

      01

      Step Up

      Artists enter. Everyone gets the same blank start and the same clock. No excuses.

      02

      Paint Live

      Start to finish in front of a crowd. No redo, no hiding the process.

      03

      The Canvas Decides

      The audience votes live, judges score the work, and a winner is named on the spot.
